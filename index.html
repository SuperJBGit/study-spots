          Help map how busy spots get

          About the data

          • Library hours come live from U of T Libraries’ LibCal hours, refreshed once a day.
          • Other hours, access rules and noise levels come from college, faculty and club pages. Each spot links its sources.
          • Spots marked hours unknown never appear in your top picks.
          • Building positions are from OpenStreetMap. Walk times assume 80 m a minute on real paths.
          • Busyness comes from other students: when you’re at a spot, the app may ask how busy it is. A report sends only the spot, your answer, the time and a random code for this phone (so one person can’t report a spot over and over). Never your location.
          • Photos are from Wikimedia Commons, Flickr (Creative Commons), U of T Libraries and our own visits, credited on each spot. Where there’s no inside photo yet, it shows the outside of the building.
